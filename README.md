I'm a postgraduate student in Development Studies at the **Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Mumbai**, spending 2026–27 on exchange at the **Paris School of International Affairs, Sciences Po**. Before that I read Political Science, with a minor in Economics, at **Lady Shri Ram College, University of Delhi**.

The question I keep returning to is a simple one with complicated answers: how do research, institutions and public policy shape the way a society works, and who ends up better or worse off because of them?

I've worked on it at three scales so far. In **Chennai**, I designed the questionnaires and interview guides for a study of a governance model the city had already put in place. At the **Bajaj Auto Foundation**, I ran field research across two CSR programmes and built the Foundation's first monitoring and evaluation framework from what it showed. At the **Ministry of External Affairs**, I compared how Germany, Denmark and Japan structure sustainability financing to India.

Away from policy work, I've written a book, ***Being Humane: To Live not Survive***, on humane living, ethical development and structural inequality.

This GitHub is where I'll keep the working side of all this (Stata and R code, datasets and policy writing) as it builds up.

### Ministry of External Affairs
**Government of India** · *Research & Data Analyst Intern* · Jul 2026<br>
*Policy Planning & Research Division*

I studied how Germany, Denmark and Japan structure sustainability financing to India. Working from more than twenty official and multilateral sources, I built a comparison matrix that scored each partner on the same criteria. Then I turned the cross-country research into a strategic report drafted for the Joint Secretary (IFS): concise briefing material from a lot of detail.

Later in the internship I extended the work to Digital Public Infrastructure and digital diplomacy, researching and writing across several themes within one portfolio.

#### Guiding Modern Bioethics: Insights from Ancient Political Thinkers
*Research paper · under review*<br>
Gene editing lets us change what was once left to nature, and it raises old political questions in a new form. Should intervention in the genetic code be limited? Will the technology deepen inequality? What regulation and responsibility should come with that power? The paper reads Plato, Aristotle and Cicero in the original texts alongside scientific literature, to see what ancient ideas of justice, equality and governance can offer the debate.<br>
[Read the paper](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1scOaMMIky2oOFnX66WaP7e52uULihmt3FNAKrJSIRk4/edit?usp=sharing)

#### Monitoring & Evaluation Indicator Framework: Service Technician Excellence Program (STEP)
*M&E framework · Bajaj Auto Foundation · 2026*<br>
An indicator framework and reporting system for STEP (Theory of Change, logic models and SMART indicators), grounded in field survey data. It covers the programme's goals, beneficiaries, delivery model and centres.<br>
[Read the work sample](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-UglaXZ25uE7XQVrXD0ilLfbkopKQxd9/view?usp=sharing)

#### Sustainability-Financing Partnerships for India: Germany, Denmark and Japan
*Comparative policy report · Ministry of External Affairs · 2026 · internal use*<br>
A comparative study of how three partners structure sustainability financing to India, submitted to the Ministry. Not publicly available.
